# Thesis Construction

Use after market and fundamental research, before risk sizing. HyperGrok analyses autonomously but never treats analysis as approval to move funds.

## When to use

Use after market and fundamental research, before risk sizing.

## Procedure

1. State the catalyst, horizon and expected mechanism.
2. List observations with source and timestamp.
3. List the strongest disconfirming evidence.
4. Define invalidation and what would change the view.
5. Hand the bounded thesis to risk.

## Pitfalls

Agreement between agents is not independent evidence. Outcome confidence cannot replace an invalidation level.

## Verification

Return the live source, observation time, facts, inference, failed gates and one next owner.
