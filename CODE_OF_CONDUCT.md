# Community guidelines

Use Discussions for installation questions, research workflows and desk setups.
Keep replies respectful and support technical claims with a source or reproduction.

Do not post secrets, signatures, wallet exports or private account payloads.
Do not promote tokens, paid signals or promised returns. HyperGrok has no token;
see the [FAQ](docs/FAQ.md#token).

Report reproducible defects through [Issues](https://github.com/galleonlabs/hypergrok-trading-desk/issues).
Report vulnerabilities privately through [Security](SECURITY.md).
Maintainers may remove spam, harassment and unsafe disclosures, or restrict
participation when these guidelines are repeatedly ignored.
